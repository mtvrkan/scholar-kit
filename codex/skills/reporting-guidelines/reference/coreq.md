# COREQ: Qualitative Research (Interviews & Focus Groups)

## Checklist (32 Items)

| # | Item | Location |
|----|------|----------|
| **1** | Research team: members, credentials, role in study | Methods: Research Team |
| **2** | Researcher characteristics: experience, qualifications, assumptions, potential bias | Methods: Reflexivity |
| **3** | Relationship with participants: prior acquaintance? Stated purpose of research? | Methods: Reflexivity |
| **4** | Ethics approval: obtained from which body, date, reference number | Methods or Ethics Statement |
| **5** | Research design: qualitative design (phenomenology, grounded theory, etc.) | Methods: Study Design |
| **6** | Theoretical framework or topic guide development: described? | Methods or Appendix |
| **7** | Sampling method: purposive, convenience, snowball, etc. | Methods: Sampling |
| **8** | Sampling frame: from which population were participants drawn? | Methods: Sampling |
| **9** | Sample size: how many; how was adequacy determined (saturation, resource limit)? | Methods: Sampling |
| **10** | Non-participation: how many were approached; reasons for non-participation (if known)? | Methods: Sampling |
| **11** | Setting: physical location of interviews/FGs (quiet room, hospital, home, etc.) | Methods: Data Collection |
| **12** | Data collection: method (interview, FG, observation) | Methods: Data Collection |
| **13** | Interview/FG guide: topics, questions, probes provided (Appendix) or described | Appendix (or Methods if detailed) |
| **14** | Repeat interviews: any participants interviewed >1 time? How many times, and interval? | Methods: Data Collection |
| **15** | Audio-recording: were interviews/FGs recorded? If not, why? | Methods: Data Collection |
| **16** | Field notes: were they taken? During or after interview? | Methods: Data Collection |
| **17** | Duration: how long were interviews/FGs (range)? | Methods: Data Collection |
| **18** | Data saturation: discussed? When did you determine saturation was reached? | Methods: Sampling or Results |
| **19** | Transcription: verbatim? Transcribed by whom? Checked against audio? | Methods: Data Management |
| **20** | Participant verification of transcripts: were transcripts returned to participants? | Methods: Data Management or Quality |

| **23** | Software: what QDA software used (NVivo, Atlas.ti, etc.) or manual coding? | Methods: Data Analysis |
| **24** | Number of initial codes: how many codes emerged before grouping into themes? | Methods: Data Analysis or Results |
| **25** | Non-coding data: how were memos, field notes, and non-interview data analyzed? | Methods: Data Analysis |

## Critical Items (Must Address)

| Item | Why Critical | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturation (18)** | Justifies sample size (N=15 with saturation is OK; N=15 without saturation is underpowered) | "Interviews continued until saturation was reached (n=22); by interviews 20–22, no new themes emerged." |
| **Quote-source chain (29–30)** | Readers verify themes are grounded in data | Every quote must have: verbatim text, participant ID, page number. Every theme ≥3 supporting quotes. |
| **Reflexivity (2, 31)** | Transparency about researcher identity and bias | "Research team: A (clinician, 15 yrs experience; potential bias toward medical model), B (newcomer; brings fresh perspective). We discussed assumptions weekly." |
| **Coding transparency (21–27)** | Readers assess reliability | "Two coders independently coded 20% of transcripts; inter-coder agreement: Cohen's κ = 0.82. Disagreements resolved by discussion." |
| **Multi-coder (21)** | Reduces single-researcher bias | Use ≥2 coders (or justify single coder: pilot study, etc.) |

## Quote-Source Chain Format

✓ **Correct:**
> **Theme: Loss of agency.** Participants described feeling excluded from decisions about their care. One stated: "Nobody asked me what I wanted to do" (P07, p. 4). Another echoed: "They decided for me without even asking" (P12, p. 8). This sense of powerlessness shaped their overall experience.

✗ **Wrong:**
> "Participants felt frustrated" (no quotes, no sources)
> "P07 said they felt frustrated" (no quote, no page)
> "One participant said 'frustrated'" (incomplete quote, no context, no page)

## Common Omissions

| Item | Missing? | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Quote page numbers | Every quote lacks page/timestamp | Add: (P##, p. XX) or (timestamp: XX:XX–XX:XX) |
| Saturation not discussed | N=10, no explanation | State: "Interviews continued until saturation; by interview X, no new themes emerged" |
| Single coder, no justification | One person analyzed all data | Use ≥2 coders or explain: "Single coder (qualified researcher with [background]); inter-coder reliability checked via [method]" |
| Reflexivity absent | No mention of researchers' backgrounds | Add: "Team: A (MD, 10 yrs clinical; potential bias toward medical framing), B (sociologist; brings critical perspective)" |
| Themes without supporting quotes | Theme stated, then moves on | Add ≥3 illustrative quotes per theme (with participant ID + page) |
| FG/interview guide not provided | No sample questions shown | Provide in Appendix: opening question, probe questions, closing |
| Data saturation vs. participant saturation | Not clear when/why sampling stopped | Specify: "Sampling continued until data saturation (n=XX); no new themes emerged in final 2–3 interviews" |
