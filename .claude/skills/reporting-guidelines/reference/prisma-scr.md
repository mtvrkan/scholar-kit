# PRISMA-ScR: Scoping Reviews

## Checklist (22 Items)

| # | Item | Location |
|----|------|----------|
| **1** | Title identifies as scoping review | Title |
| **2** | Structured abstract (background, objectives, methods, results, conclusion) | Abstract |
| **3** | Rationale: why conduct a scoping review? (vs. systematic review) | Introduction |
| **4a** | Research question(s): PEO/PIO framework | Introduction (end) or Methods |
| **4b** | Research question(s) PICOS / PEO elements specified | Introduction or Methods |
| **5a** | Information sources: databases, registers, websites, contact with experts | Methods: Search |
| **5b** | Date of information sources (e.g., searched Jan 1–Dec 31, 2023) | Methods: Search |
| **6a** | Search strategy designed by: librarian, research team? | Methods: Search |
| **6b** | Full search strategy for ≥1 database (keywords, MeSH, limits) | Methods: Search or Appendix |
| **7** | Inclusion/exclusion criteria: explicit for study characteristics | Methods: Study Selection |
| **8** | Data extraction form: what data were extracted? (author, year, design, population, outcomes, key findings) | Methods: Data Extraction or Appendix |
| **9a** | Study selection process: how many screeners? Independent? | Methods: Study Selection |
| **9b** | Any disagreement resolution (consensus, third reviewer) | Methods: Study Selection |
| **10a** | Characteristics of included studies (author, year, country, design, population, outcomes) | Results: Table 1 (Study Characteristics) |
| **10b** | Mapping: studies organized by key dimensions (theme × design type, population, outcome) | Results: Mapping Table or Figure |
| **11** | Summary of key findings by theme or dimension | Results |
| **12a** | Certainty of evidence: quality of studies (if assessed) | Results or Discussion |
| **12b** | Publication bias: considered? (if applicable) | Discussion |
| **13** | Methodological gaps in the evidence (what is not studied?) | Discussion |
| **14** | Overall findings interpretation in context of existing knowledge | Discussion |
| **15** | Implications for future research and practice | Discussion |
| **16** | Limitations of the scoping review (search limits, scope limits, bias risk) | Discussion: Limitations |
| **17** | Funding sources and conflicts of interest | Funding statement |

---

## Key Difference from Systematic Review

| Aspect | Systematic Review | Scoping Review |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Answer a focused question (does X work?) | Map the field (what exists, what's missing) |
| **Search** | Comprehensive, reproducible | Comprehensive but may be selective |
| **Inclusion** | Narrow criteria to test specific hypothesis | Broad criteria to capture landscape |
| **Quality assessment** | Always; affects synthesis | Optional |
| **Meta-analysis** | Often | Rare |
| **Outcome** | Effect size, evidence certainty | Mapping table, gap identification |

---

## Mapping Table (Critical)

Shows which studies address which dimensions:

| Author, Year | Study Type | Population | Intervention | Outcome | Key Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smith et al., 2022 | RCT | Adults 18–65 | X method | Y outcome | Improved by 10% |
| Jones et al., 2021 | Cohort | Older adults | X method | Z outcome | No effect |
| Patel et al., 2020 | Qualitative | Adolescents | X method | Implementation barriers | 3 barriers identified |

**Purpose:** Readers see at a glance which populations, outcomes, and study designs are covered; which gaps exist (e.g., "No studies in pediatric population" or "Y outcome never measured").

---

## Scoping vs. Depth (Item 3)

**State clearly why scoping review, not systematic review:**

**Examples:**
- "We chose a scoping review to map the breadth of evidence, as the field is emerging and diverse in methods and outcomes. A systematic review would be premature."
- "We conducted a scoping review to identify research gaps before designing a systematic review protocol."
- "The landscape of X includes many study designs and populations; a scoping review was appropriate to characterize this diversity."

## Common Omissions

| Item | Missing? | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Mapping table | "Results are summarized in the text" | Add: Table showing studies × dimensions (design, population, outcome) |
| Search strategy | Databases listed, but exact queries not provided | Show: keywords, MeSH terms, filters per database in Appendix |
| Inclusion criteria | Vague: "relevant studies" | Specify: Population (who?), Intervention (what?), Outcome (which?), Context, Study design (which types?) |
| Scope justification | Not explained why scoping review chosen | State: "We chose scoping review to map [X topic] landscape, as the field is diverse" |
| Quality assessment | No mention of whether included studies' quality was assessed | State: "We did not assess quality because [reason]" OR "We assessed quality using [tool]" |
| Limitations of search | No language/date/design restrictions mentioned | Acknowledge: "We limited to English-language articles published since 2010; non-English evidence may exist" |
